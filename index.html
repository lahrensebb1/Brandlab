Good questions

Before you ask —

What does a website cost?

Fixed prices, no vague quotes: a Startsite is €950, a Bedrijfssite €1.750 and a Groeisite €2.850. Bigger or more complex projects start from €3.750 with a custom quote. All prices ex. VAT.

How long does it take?

Most sites launch within a few weeks. The biggest factor is usually how quickly we get your content — we keep the process tight with a clear plan and fixed revision rounds.

What do you need from me?

Whatever you already have: logo, photos, texts and access to your domain. Don't worry if it's incomplete — we guide the structure and help shape the message. We do ask for one decision-maker, so feedback stays fast and clear.

I don't have a logo or branding yet.

No problem. A Brand Refresh Light (from €650) gives you a polished logo, colours, typography and templates — and pairs perfectly with a new website.

Who maintains the site after launch?

We can. Care plans start at €39/month for hosting, updates, backups and small edits — up to €129/month with monthly improvement checks. You never have to think about it.

How does payment work?

Simple and upfront. Smaller projects: 60% to start, 40% before launch. Larger projects: 50% to start, 40% after design approval, 10% before launch. Monthly plans are billed in advance.

What about content after launch?

That's the Grow part. Monthly content plans start at €595/month for posts and planning, with video content plans from €1.000/month — so your business stays visible after the site goes live.
